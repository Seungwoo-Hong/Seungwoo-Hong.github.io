## Join Us
We are always looking for motivated graduate students, postdocs, and student assistants interested in developing physical intelligence for real-world robotics applications—including, but not limited to, hardware design, planning and control algorithms (model-based and/or learning-based).

If you are interested in joining our lab, please email me and include the following as a single PDF:
- **Cover Letter**: Explain your motivation, why you are interested in our lab, and how you would contribute to our research (max. 1-2 page).
- **Curriculum Vitae (CV)**: Include relevant projects, research experience, and/or industry experience.
- **Academic Transcript**: Include your most recent grade transcript (for students).
- **Research Statement**: Include your research accomplishments, current work, and future direction and potential of your work (for postdocs).
- **Additional Materials (Optional)**: Any other documents or information you would like to share.
